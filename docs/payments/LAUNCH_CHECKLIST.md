# Payments Launch Checklist

## Current launch blockers

- general double-entry ledger foundation missing
- immutable financial snapshot missing
- reservation state machine missing
- unified payout foundation missing
- unified reconciliation workflow missing

## Before pilot

- confirm live Paystack credentials in the deployment environment
- confirm live feature flags for customer wallet and DVA
- confirm webhook signature processing on the deployed site
- confirm customer DVA provisioning on a controlled account
- confirm wallet spend and refund paths in staging or controlled live testing

## Before public launch

- confirm all pilot items
- complete low-value live-mode tests
- verify duplicate webhook protection
- verify payout kill switch
- verify reconciliation and rollback steps

## Status

- Not launch ready yet.
